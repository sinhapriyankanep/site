About Me

Welcome to my page!

I am Priyanka, a recent graduate in Aerospace Engineering from the Israel Institute of Technology (Technion). Currently, I am engaged in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) analysis of synthetic jet impingement for various waveforms at the Fluid Mechanics and Machinery Lab, Indian Institute of Technology, Indore. My research specializes in advanced measurement techniques for thermo-fluid dynamics, multiphase flows, and the fundamental fluid mechanics governing liquid sheet jets.

Feel free to explore my site to learn more about my research, projects, and areas of interest.

Research

Hypersonic Leading Edges

My MSc project involves developing cooling techniques for hypersonic vehicle leading edges. I focus on creating liquid sheet jets to achieve efficient cooling, utilizing advanced experimental facilities and shadowgraph imaging for precise characterization.

Liquid Sheet Jet Dynamics

I investigate the dynamics of liquid sheets generated by polyimide sheet nozzles, analyzing the impact of nozzle irregularities on sheet morphology. This research has significant implications for cooling systems in extreme environments.
